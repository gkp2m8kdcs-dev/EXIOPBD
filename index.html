Frequently asked.

If something isn't covered here, head to Support.

Does EXIO PBD use real money or connect to a brokerage?

No. EXIO PBD is a self-contained simulator. All prices, fills, NAV and P&L are generated locally by a deterministic random-walk engine. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no real-money trading path.

What data does the app collect?

None. The app stores your positions, orders, alerts and price history as local JSON files on your device. It uses no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising identifiers. See the Privacy Policy for the full disclosure.

Can I pause or reset the simulator?

Yes. The Settings tab lets you pause the live feed, change starting cash, reset the book back to seed, or wipe all local data.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The simulator, charts, order entry, alerts and analytics all run on-device.

Which platforms are supported?

iOS, with a SwiftUI interface that adapts from iPhone to iPad. iOS 17 or later is recommended.
