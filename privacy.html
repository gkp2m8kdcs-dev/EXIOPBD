Privacy Policy

EXIO PBD is a self-contained trading-desk simulator. The short version: we do not collect personal data, we do not track you, and the app does not talk to any server.

Last updated: January 2026

Summary. EXIO PBD runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers. Your positions, orders, alerts and price history are stored locally as JSON files and never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

EXIO PBD does not collect personal information from you. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
  • Account credentials — there are no accounts.
  • Location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera input.
  • Device identifiers used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports tied to a user identity, or behavioral profiles.
  • Financial information — the simulator does not handle real money.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device. It is never transmitted off the device and can be deleted at any time from the Settings tab or by uninstalling the app:

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Positions, working orders, fills Resume your desk between sessions. Local JSON inside the app sandbox.
Price history & notifications Render charts, alerts and the live feed. Local JSON inside the app sandbox.
App preferences (e.g. starting cash) Restore your setup on next launch. UserDefaults (standard).

All on-device storage is governed by iOS sandboxing — no other app, and no remote service, can read it.

3. Permissions the App Uses

EXIO PBD requests no runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.).

4. Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, ad networks, or social-login components. It does not call any remote endpoint. There is no server-side component operated by us.

5. Children's Privacy

EXIO PBD is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Because the app collects no personal data, the protections of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent frameworks are not implicated.

6. Data Security

Because the app collects nothing and transmits nothing, the principal security boundaries are the iOS sandbox and your device's passcode / Face ID. We recommend keeping your device locked and your OS up to date. If you choose to use iCloud or device backups, those backups are governed by Apple's policies and your iCloud settings.

7. International Users

EXIO PBD is operated from a single, local-only architecture: your device. There is no cross-border data transfer because there is no data transfer at all.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, port, or delete on a server. On your device you can at any time:

  • Open Settings → Reset in the app to clear all stored data.
  • Uninstall the app to remove it and its sandbox entirely.
  • Disable iCloud backup of the app if you do not want it included in backups.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way (for example, by adding a feature that does transmit data), we will update the "Last updated" date above and surface the change inside the app before it takes effect.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app's data practices, please contact us via the Support page.
