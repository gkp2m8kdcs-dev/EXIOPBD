Frequently asked questions

The price for one of my instruments isn't moving. Is the feed stuck?

First, check the top bar — the live indicator should show a green dot. If it does and a particular ticker is flat, that's normal: the engine is a deterministic random walk, and a flat stretch is a possible state. You can also pause/resume the feed from Settings, or tap the price card to force a reset of just that ticker.

My positions disappeared after I reinstalled the app.

Positions, orders, fills, alerts and notifications are stored locally in the app sandbox. Uninstalling removes them. To preserve state across reinstalls, keep the app installed, or use the device's iCloud backup with the app included.

How do I reset the simulator back to a clean book?

Open Settings → Reset desk. You'll be asked to confirm. This clears all positions, orders, alerts, fills and price history, and reseeds the random walk from the base prices.

An alert isn't firing when I expect it to.

Alerts evaluate against the last price at each tick. Check the alert direction (above / below) and the current value — alerts only trigger on a crossing, not on being already-past the threshold. You can verify the active alerts list from the Notifications tab.

Does the app work in landscape on iPad?

Yes. The desk, markets and analytics layouts adapt to landscape on iPhone and iPad.

Can I export my data?

The app stores JSON files inside its sandbox. If you need an export for support, use the iOS "Files" share sheet from the Settings tab to share a copy with yourself.

Will the app sync between my devices?

Not today. EXIO PBD keeps state local to each device. We may add optional iCloud sync in a future version; if we do, it will be opt-in and surfaced in-app.

I have a feature / instrument I want traded on the desk.

Send it to ideas@exio-pbd.example. The current instrument set is curated; new tickers are added periodically.

Known limitations

  • The simulator is single-device; there is no multi-user book or shared ledger.
  • No backtesting module is shipped in this version.
  • No real-money or brokerage connection. The app is a study environment, not a trading platform.
